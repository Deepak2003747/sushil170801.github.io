ONESTAR MILK ANALYZERS

Dairy society record-management app by Onestar Private Limited

ONESTAR MILK ANALYZERS is a mobile application built for dairy societies to record and manage daily milk collection, customer accounts, and payments. It is designed to work fully offline on a phone at the collection center, with an optional cloud backup feature.

What the app does

  • Records daily milk collection entries for each customer, including FAT, SNF, weight, and price
  • Manages customer records, codes, and phone numbers
  • Tracks payments and purchase entries for the dairy society
  • Prints collection receipts using a connected thermal printer
  • Supports rate charts to automatically calculate milk price based on FAT/SNF
  • Works fully offline; all data is stored locally on the device by default

Optional Google Backup & Sync

Users may optionally connect their own Google account so the app can keep an automatic backup of their records in a Google Sheet stored in their own Google Drive. This makes it possible to restore all data if a phone is lost, damaged, or reset. This feature is entirely optional and the app works without it.
