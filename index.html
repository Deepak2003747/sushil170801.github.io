How it works

Record the entry

Enter the customer's weight, FAT, and SNF readings from the analyzer — works with no signal, no internet.

Rate calculates automatically

The app looks up the price from your society's rate chart based on FAT/SNF, so nobody has to do the math by hand.

Receipt prints on the spot

Send the entry straight to a connected thermal printer — the customer walks away with a receipt immediately.

Backup runs quietly (optional)

If you've connected a Google account, the day's records sync to a Sheet in your own Drive — nothing is lost if the phone is damaged or replaced.
